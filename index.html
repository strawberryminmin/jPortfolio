Jasmine Tripathy 🎀

Hi, I'm Jasmine Tripathy, a Computer Science Engineering student with a keen interest in AI/ML and Generative AI. I enjoy building practical applications, experimenting with new technologies, and turning ideas into projects that are actually useful (and occasionally debugging them for much longer than expected).

My work so far has involved Machine Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, LLMs, LangChain, RAG, and AI-powered applications. I'm currently exploring AI agents, MCP, and LLM-based applications while continuing to build and learn along the way.

When I'm away from the code, you'll probably find me reading, watching anime or K-dramas, exploring cafés, listening to BTS, or getting unnecessarily excited about Hello Kitty. In short: I like learning, I like building, and I'm usually curious about what I can make next.
